# Example 1

## Justin Welsh

Publish frequently to build consistency.

## Richard van der Blom

Publishing frequently is useless if the content doesn't build authority.

# My conclusion

I agree more with Richard. Consistency matters, but publishing low-value content simply to maintain a schedule can damage credibility. A consistent cadence combined with expertise is more sustainable than chasing volume.

#Example 2

## Ross Simmonds

Repurpose content across many channels.

## Amanda Natividad

Focus first on creating assets people will actually forward internally.

# My conclusion

Distribution is important, but distribution amplifies quality. If the original content isn't useful enough to be shared inside companies, repurposing it won't solve the problem.

# Example 3
