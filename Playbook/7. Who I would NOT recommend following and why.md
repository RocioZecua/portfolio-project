# Who I Would NOT Recommend Following and Why

## Chris Walker (Based on the Material Reviewed)

Chris Walker is a highly respected B2B marketing professional, and I would not discourage people from following his work in general. However, based on the material I collected for this research project, I would not recommend his content as a primary resource for someone looking to build an organic LinkedIn strategy for B2B SaaS.

The content I reviewed focused primarily on the philosophy of open-source software, including topics such as community governance, transparency, and software freedom. While these are valuable discussions, they do not provide actionable guidance for LinkedIn content strategy, audience building, content distribution, or demand generation.

For the specific objective of this playbook, I found the work of experts such as Justin Welsh, Richard van der Blom, Amanda Natividad, and Ross Simmonds to be significantly more relevant because they provide practical frameworks that can be directly applied to LinkedIn marketing.

This decision is not a criticism of Chris Walker's expertise. Rather, it reflects the importance of selecting sources that closely match the goals and scope of the research.
